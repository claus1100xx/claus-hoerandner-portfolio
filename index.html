About

Two decades of insurance finance — and a practical taste for change.

I lead holistic finance transformation at Zurich North America, modernising processes, tools, and ways of working across every finance department. The goal is simple: faster close cycles, cleaner data, and decision-ready reporting for the people who run the business.

My work bridges complex financial standards — the IFRS 17 transition, statutory reporting, enterprise planning — with the tools that make them workable: Power BI, Alteryx, Blue Prism RPA, and the first generation of finance AI with Copilot and Zurich's internal assistant.

I've trained 500+ finance professionals through Lunch & Learns, short-form video, and applied demonstrations. Bilingual in English and German. Summa Cum Laude graduate; CPCU, AIAF, and Six Sigma Green Belt; board-level nonprofit leader.
